Equations used

Confidence Building = Confidence Building Rate × Public Confidence Confidence Erosion Rate = Embedded erosion coefficient × Adverse System Perception Confidence Erosion = Confidence Erosion Rate × Public Confidence dC/dt = Confidence Building − Confidence Erosion Exact solution: C(t) = C₀ × exp[(building rate − erosion rate) × t]

Only adverse perception differs between the three presets. Initial confidence, rate coefficients, horizon and method are shared. Preset names describe the original parameter settings; editing shared rates can change their behaviour.

The initial value 0.1816 was reconstructed from published survey response counts. Rate coefficients and adverse-perception values are illustrative assumptions. These outputs are not empirical forecasts. This proportional-flow model has no upper bound and does not generate growth followed by saturation.
